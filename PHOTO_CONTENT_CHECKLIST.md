# Maa Ki Rasoi (माँ की रसोई) · Content & Photography Checklist

To maintain the high-end boutique brand integrity and comply with our zero-stock, zero-AI food policy, all photography must feature real food cooked in your Borkhera kitchen.

---

## Shot List & Specifications

### Shot 01: Signature Ghar Ki Thali (Primary Hero & Chapter 1)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 Vertical (will be displayed inside an architectural arch window)
- **Props & Dishware:** Traditional kansa (bell metal) or clean stainless steel thali.
- **Dishes:**
  - 4 puffed wheat phulkas brushed with glistening pure desi ghee (stacked naturally)
  - Yellow arhar dal tadka with visible cumin seeds and whole dried red chili in a small brass katori
  - Dry seasonal sabzi (e.g. aloo gobhi or bhindi masala)
  - Steamed long-grain basmati rice
  - Fresh sliced red onions, green chili and lemon wedge
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight from side window (morning 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM). Visible soft steam rising from dal and rotis.
- **Note:** No oil sprays, no fake garnishes. Authentic homemade warmth.

---

### Shot 02: Tawa Action & Phulka Puffing (Chapter 2 Roti & Sabzi)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 or 1:1 Square
- **Action:** Cook's hands gently lifting a puffed, ballooned phulka directly off the heavy iron tawa.
- **Focus:** Crisp steam release, golden charred blisters on wheat, brush coating of desi ghee.
- **Mood:** Warm, artisan hearth, honest craftsmanship.

---

### Shot 03: Slow-Simmered Dal & Mandi Spices (Chapter 3 Dal Chawal)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape or 4:3
- **Focus:** Sizzling brass tadka ladle pouring ghee, cumin, crushed garlic and hing over steaming yellow dal.
- **Background:** Raw clay bowls containing whole cumin, coriander seeds, and dried red chilies.

### Shot 04: Food-Grade Tiffin Packaging (Chapter 4 Tiffin Plans)

## Photography Rules & Tips
1. **Time of Day:** Shoot between 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM when natural Rajasthan sunlight provides soft, non-directional illumination.
2. **Camera:** An iPhone 13/14/15/16 Pro (using 2x portrait lens) or DSLR (35mm / 50mm f/1.8 lens).
3. **No Artificial Props:** Do not buy plastic leaves, fake condensation spray, or neon lamps.
4. **Cleanliness:** Wipe dish rims with a clean linen cloth before pressing the shutter.
